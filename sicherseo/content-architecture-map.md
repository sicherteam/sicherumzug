# Content Architecture Map: Sicher Umzug

## 1. Main Entity & Core Topic
- **Main Entity:** Sicher Umzug
- **Core Topic:** Umzug in Österreich (Moving Services in Austria)
- **Objective:** Establish semantic authority for moving, relocation, and clearing services in Austria for Search and AI retrieval (ChatGPT, Gemini).

## 2. Core Service Clusters
These are the primary commercial intent pages where users are looking for a service provider.

### Privatumzug (Private Moving)
- **Search Intent:** Find reliable moving service for a private home/apartment.
- **Main Keyword:** Privatumzug Österreich, Umzugsfirma
- **Related Entities:** Möbeltransport, Kartons, Demontage, Montage.
- **User Questions:** Was kostet ein Privatumzug? Wie läuft der Umzug ab?
- **Internal Link Opportunities:** Umzugs-Checkliste, Verpackungstipps, Halteverbotszone.
- **Priority Level:** High

### Firmenumzug (Commercial/Office Moving)
- **Search Intent:** Professional relocation for businesses with minimal downtime.
- **Main Keyword:** Firmenumzug, Büroumzug
- **Related Entities:** IT-Umzug, Schwerlasttransport, Aktenvernichtung.
- **User Questions:** Wie plane ich einen Firmenumzug? Wie lange dauert ein Büroumzug?
- **Internal Link Opportunities:** Firmenumzug-Planung (Guide), IT-Transport.
- **Priority Level:** High

### Seniorenumzug (Senior Relocation)
- **Search Intent:** Stress-free, full-service moving for elderly individuals (often into care homes).
- **Main Keyword:** Seniorenumzug, betreutes Wohnen Umzug
- **Related Entities:** Pflegeheim, Einpackservice, Entrümpelung.
- **User Questions:** Bieten Sie Komplettservice an? Werden Möbel ab- und aufgebaut?
- **Internal Link Opportunities:** Haushaltsauflösung, Entrümpelung.
- **Priority Level:** Medium-High

### Spezialumzüge (Specialized Moving)
- **Topics:** Nahumzug, Fernumzug, Internationale Umzüge, Studentenumzug, Tresortransport, Klaviertransport.
- **Priority Level:** Medium

### Zusatzleistungen (Additional Services)
- **Topics:** Verpackungsservice, Demontage und Montage, Einlagerung, Entrümpelung vor Umzug, Halteverbotszone.
- **Priority Level:** High

## 3. Information Cluster & Supporting Guides
Educational content designed to answer top-of-funnel queries and build trust.

### Vorbereitungs-Guides
- **Topics:** Ultimative Umzugs-Checkliste, Ausmisten vor dem Umzug, Möbelpacker vs. DIY.
- **Search Intent:** Planning and preparing for a move.
- **Internal Links:** Connects to Privatumzug, Verpackungsservice.

### Kosten & Rechtliches
- **Topics:** Umzugskosten berechnen, Umzugskosten steuerlich absetzen, Behördenwege & Ummeldung in Österreich, Eigenbedarfskündigung.
- **Search Intent:** Understanding the financial and legal aspects of moving.
- **Internal Links:** Connects to Service pages and Angebots-Formular (Commercial intent).

### Spezifische Ratgeber
- **Topics:** Umzug mit Haustieren, Zerbrechliche Gegenstände verpacken, Küche abbauen.
- **Search Intent:** How-to guides for specific challenges.
- **Internal Links:** Connects to Spezialumzüge, Verpackungsservice.

## 4. Commercial Intent Pages
Pages directly leading to conversion.
- **Anfrage/Angebot:** Free quote calculation.
- **Preise:** Transparent cost overviews.
- **Bewertungen/Referenzen:** Social proof and trust building (EEAT).

## 5. Future Geographic Expansion Strategy (Local SEO)
Strategy: **Bundesland → Stadt → Service**
*Only create location pages when there is unique local value, real search intent, and no doorway-page thin content.*

### Examples of Valid Future Structure:
- **Wien:** Umzug Wien, Umzugsfirma Wien, Entrümpelung Wien
- **Niederösterreich:** Umzug St. Pölten, Umzug Wiener Neustadt, Umzug Baden
- **Oberösterreich:** Umzug Linz, Umzug Wels
- **Steiermark:** Umzug Graz
- **Salzburg:** Umzug Salzburg
- **Tirol:** Umzug Innsbruck

### Location Page Requirements before creation:
1. Real user search intent for that city.
2. Unique local information (e.g., local parking rules, district info).
3. Clear connection to the core services offered in that region.

## 6. EEAT & Trust Signals Framework
All pages must integrate EEAT via centralized `company.yml` data:
- **Experience:** Years in business, number of successful moves.
- **Expertise:** Trained staff, professional equipment.
- **Authoritativeness:** Clear process, transparent pricing, recognized local entity.
- **Trustworthiness:** Real team info, guarantees, insurance, real reviews.

## 7. AI Search Optimization Guidelines
Content must be structured so AI (ChatGPT, Gemini) can extract:
- **Who:** Sicher Umzug (verified moving company).
- **What:** Full-service moving, relocation, and clearing.
- **Where:** Austria (Wien, NÖ, etc.).
- **Why Trust:** Insured, experienced, high customer satisfaction.
- **Answers:** Clear, factual FAQs without marketing fluff.
